Get into the booth

01

Download your purchase

On your Android device, open your Payhip receipt and download the APK. Keep your receipt and license key private. The app is supplied directly as an APK, not through Google Play.

02

Install Afterdark

Open the APK in Downloads. If Android asks, allow installation for the browser or file manager you used. You can turn that permission off afterward. If a security warning flags the file, contact support before continuing.

03

Activate online

Open Afterdark while connected to the internet. Enter the license key from your receipt, then choose Enter the Booth after activation. Your license supports two devices.

04

Bring your music

Rotate horizontally. Open Library → Music folder. Select your music folder and choose Use this folder; subfolders are included. Use Files when you only want individual tracks. Pause a deck before replacing its track.

Make your first transition

A

Start with one track

Load a familiar track on Deck A and move the crossfader toward A. Press PLAY. Load a second track on B while that deck is paused.

B

Bring in the next track

Start B, then move the crossfader gradually toward it. Listen for clashes and adjust the EQ. Mix Next can suggest pairings and audition transitions; use your ears to decide what works.

FX

Try one technique at a time

Drag a record to scratch. Open FX to experiment with an effect, then switch it off to hear the difference. Practice a short passage before combining scratching, effects and a transition. Wired audio is preferable when timing matters.

Keep the good take

  1. In Record & Share, choose audio or portrait video. Try audio first; video needs more processing power.
  2. Tap REC, perform a short mix, then tap STOP.
  3. Wait for preparation. Choose a destination in Android’s Save dialog and finish saving before leaving the app.
  4. If you cancel the dialog, open Library → Recover last recording.

Export your recordings before uninstalling or changing devices. Private app data, cues and folder permissions do not automatically transfer. Only share recordings you have permission to distribute.

If something gets in the way

My music is missing

Select the folder again in Library and confirm the files are still stored there. Use Files to try an individual track. Streaming-library entries are not local audio files.

Activation failed or both device slots are occupied

Check your internet connection and the key in your receipt. Pause both decks and finish recording/export before opening License & Devices. Deactivate an old device online before replacing it. If an error persists, contact support with your order ID and exact error; do not repeatedly retry an uncertain activation.

How does offline use work?

Internet is required for activation and license refresh. After successful validation, offline access lasts up to 30 days. Refresh before that period ends when an internet connection is available.

The audio glitches or recording is slow

Try audio recording instead of video, reduce visual effects and close demanding apps. Keep Afterdark in the foreground. Performance varies by device; Android 9+ is the minimum requirement, not a guarantee for every phone.

Can I use Spotify, YouTube or an iPhone?

This edition uses your local music files on Android. Music, Spotify/YouTube streaming, stem separation and key lock are not included. An iPhone app or Windows license is not included in this purchase.

I need help with an update

Contact support before uninstalling so you can protect your recordings. Include the installed app version, phone model, Android version and exact message shown.

Need a hand?

Send your order ID, app version, device model, Android version and exact error. Never send passwords or post your full license key publicly.
