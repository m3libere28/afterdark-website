GETTING STARTED

From folder to first session.

  1. Bring your tracks

    Use Library → Music folder to select local audio and its subfolders. Load one track on each deck.

  2. Find your transition

    Listen, adjust the crossfader and EQ, then try cues, scratching and effects. Audition suggestions before committing.

  3. Keep the take

    Choose audio or portrait video, tap REC, then STOP. Wait for preparation and save through Android’s file picker.

Devices and compatibility

The current build requires Android 9 or later and is designed for landscape use. Physical-device acceptance testing is ongoing; a final supported-device list is not available yet. Keep the app in the foreground while mixing. Video recording requires more processing power.

Music sources and permissions

Afterdark works with local audio files you supply. Commercial music is not included. Spotify and YouTube streaming, stem separation and key lock are not included in this edition. Only share recordings you have permission to distribute.

Saving and changing devices

If a save dialog is cancelled, use Recover last recording in Library. Export recordings before uninstalling or changing devices. Private app data, cues and folder permissions do not automatically transfer.

PROJECT STATUS

Building toward release.

Afterdark is an independent project developed by an individual based in Spain. Automated build and regression checks are underway alongside licensing integration. Real-device testing and purchase-flow verification remain before commercial release.
